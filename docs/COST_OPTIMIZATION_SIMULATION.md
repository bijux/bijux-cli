# Cost optimization simulation scenarios

## Simulation families

- cross-region replay with egress-heavy paths
- hot-cache miss causing recompute bursts
- bursty backfill under constrained budget policies

## Evaluation outputs

- projected cost envelope
- budget policy action distribution (allow/throttle/reroute)
- cost-performance profile outcomes (cheapest-safe, balanced, fastest-safe)
- safety policy compliance status

## Acceptance criteria

A scenario passes only when optimization decisions remain within determinism, trust, and compliance boundaries.
