# Performance Optimization Checklist

1. Graph parsing and DAG validation benchmarks are present and up to date.
2. Planner and scheduler latency benchmarks are present and reviewed.
3. Runtime node overhead and resource profiling reports are generated.
4. Artifact hash and artifact IO throughput reports are generated.
5. Replay, diff, explain, and run history latency reports are generated.
6. Provenance and artifact store benchmark coverage is available.
7. Regression thresholds and trend summaries are updated.
8. No unresolved release-blocking regressions remain in benchmark governance outputs.
