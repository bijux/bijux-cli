- strengthen machine-checkable invariants for scheduler idempotence and policy deny finality

## Mid term

- deepen model-based coverage for run/node transitions and HA ownership transitions

## Long term

- broaden formally constrained surfaces for trust-critical execution and provenance guarantees
- harden verification-driven release governance across all platform subsystems
