# What We Do Not Do

- We do not claim “feature complete” unless the command parity matrix confirms it.
- We do not claim “Rust-first complete” unless parity and packaging convergence are both evidenced.
- We do not add new stateful features before state parity and diagnostics rules are in place.
- We do not use separate command-law logic in the Python bridge.
- We do not treat vanity test totals as quality evidence.
- We do not keep overlapping long-form docs when generated artifacts can carry the detail.
