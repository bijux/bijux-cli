# What We Won't Claim

1. We won't claim global completion while parity matrix rows are `missing` or `partial`.
2. We won't claim runtime convergence without runtime identity and packaging evidence.
3. We won't claim plugin lifecycle completion while lifecycle commands remain partial or gaps remain open.
4. We won't claim documentation convergence without generated docs-audit evidence.
5. We won't claim test quality convergence without weak-test audit evidence.

Primary evidence:
- `artifacts/parity/command_parity_matrix.json`
- `artifacts/status/runtime_unity_report.json`
- `artifacts/status/plugin_state_report.json`
- `artifacts/status/docs_audit.json`
- `artifacts/status/test_quality_audit.json`
